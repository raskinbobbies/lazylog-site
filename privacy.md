# LazyLog Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

LazyLog is a fitness and food-logging app for iPhone made by the LazyLog team ("we"). This policy explains what data LazyLog handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: **your logs live on your phone, there is no account, and we only send data off your phone when a feature needs it to work.**

## What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and are never uploaded by LazyLog:

- your profile and goals (age, height, weight, sex, targets)
- meals you log, their photos, saved meals and recipes
- workouts, weight, sleep and step history
- progress photos (protected by Face ID and kept out of your photo library)

Backups and exports you create are files you control. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple handles that copy under its own terms.

**Apple Health.** If you allow it, LazyLog reads data such as steps from Apple Health. Health data is used on your device only and is never used for advertising or sold.

## What leaves your phone, and why

LazyLog uses a small server we run on Cloudflare ("the LazyLog server") to reach AI and places services. It sends data only when you use these features:

| Feature | What is sent | Who processes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meal estimates | the meal photo, your description, or your voice recording | LazyLog server, then OpenAI |
| Voice entry | audio while you hold the mic | LazyLog server, then OpenAI |
| Recipe import | a recipe's caption or ingredient lines (the web page itself is read on your phone) | LazyLog server, then OpenAI |
| Guidance and suggestions | a summary of your day and recent history: calories and protein so far, goal, recent workouts, sleep and weight | LazyLog server, then OpenAI |
| Find food nearby | your current location when you ask, and your remaining calories and protein | LazyLog server, Google Places and OpenAI |
| Feedback | what you write, your app version, iOS version and device model, and a photo or diagnostics only if you attach them | LazyLog server |
| Usage counts (off by default, can be turned on) | daily counts only, such as whether the app was opened, how many meals were logged, how many AI estimates were made or edited, workouts completed and recipes imported, plus app and iOS version | LazyLog server |

**Usage counts** help us see whether LazyLog is useful day to day. They never include what you ate, photos, food names, calories, weight, health data, location or times of day. They are off unless you turn on "Share anonymous usage counts" in Settings. Turning it off again deletes anything not yet sent from your phone.

Every request carries a random **installation ID** created on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and we use it only to apply fair-use limits and prevent abuse. It is stored in your phone's keychain, so it can stay on the device after the app is deleted.

## What we keep

- **AI allowance counters:** how many AI requests an installation ID made each day, to enforce the daily limit.
- **Usage counts:** the daily counts described above, kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.
- **Recent estimates:** the result of a meal estimate (not the photo) is kept for up to 7 days so a retried request does not count twice, then deleted.
- **Feedback:** messages and any attachments you choose to send are kept until we have dealt with them. Ask us to delete them at any time.

We do not keep your meal photos, voice recordings or recipe text after a request is answered. We call OpenAI with storage turned off. OpenAI may keep request data for a limited time for abuse monitoring under its [API data usage policy](https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies). Google processes location searches under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we do not do

- No accounts, no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell or share your data for marketing.
- We do not use your data to train AI models.

## Your choices

- Use LazyLog without any AI feature. Typing a meal's numbers yourself, workouts and history all work without sending anything.
- Deny or revoke camera, microphone, location, Apple Health and notification access in iOS Settings at any time.
- Delete all your data by deleting the app. Contact us to delete feedback you sent.

## Age

LazyLog is for adults aged 18 and over. Calorie and protein targets are estimates for planning, not medical advice.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes. Questions or deletion requests: [lazylog.support@proton.me](mailto:lazylog.support@proton.me).
