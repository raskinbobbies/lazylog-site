# LazyLog Support

## Get in touch

Email [lazylog.support@proton.me](mailto:lazylog.support@proton.me). We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

You can also write to us from the app: go to **Profile › Settings › Send feedback**. Use it for ideas, wishes, or anything that doesn't work as it should. You can attach a screenshot if it helps.

## Back up your data

LazyLog has no account, so your logs live only on your phone. To keep a copy:

1. Go to **Profile › Settings › Your data › Back up your data**.
2. Save the backup file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or Files.

A backup holds your records, saved meals and recipes with their photos, and your progress photos. To bring it back, for example on a new phone, use **Restore a backup** in the same place.

## Delete your data

- **In the app:** go to **Profile › Settings › Start over › Delete all data**. This removes everything you logged and saved and keeps your settings.
- **Everything at once:** delete the LazyLog app from your phone.
- **Feedback you sent us:** email [lazylog.support@proton.me](mailto:lazylog.support@proton.me) and we'll delete it.

LazyLog doesn't keep your meal photos, voice recordings or recipe text on its server after a request is answered. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for the details.
